Work in progress & unfinished Github projects
AD57xx arduino library: working fork of paulvha's AD57xx that supports multiple instances of AD57xx on SPI ports different from the standard, like SPI1 on RP2040. Lacks documentation and testing :(
Cheap, microcontroller based Lock-In amplifier: works but lacks hardware description and working higher order filters. The python based GUI is very slow at this moment and will be transferred from tkinter to pyQt. This is my top priority from this list and i hope to find time within the next year to finish it!
PhotocurrentAmplifiery: Hardware project containing circuit, PCB and component information for a very sensitive current to voltage amplifier (transimpedance amplifier = TIA). Input referred noise levels can be in the pA regime. A basic design for quieting a switch noise power supplies output is also contained in the project and called LowNoisePSU. The photo diode amplifier based on this TIA is not yet fully documented, thats why its in the "work in progress" category. The design was developed during my Physics bachelor thesis at the Institute of Experimental Physics (IEP) at TU Graz. I will make my Bachelor thesis available here at some point
